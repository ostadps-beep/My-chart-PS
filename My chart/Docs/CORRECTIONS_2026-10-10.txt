══ 3. C2 — ZOOM / PRICE-SCALE DEFECT (root cause found, revert to spec behaviour) ══
SYMPTOM: zoom/pan feels wrong; zoom on the price axis and vertical drag do nothing or snap back.
CAUSE (MyChart.UI/Chart/ChartHost.xaml.cs):
  - FitPriceToVisible() is called after EVERY mouse wheel, after EVERY drag move and inside
    UpdateSize() (so on every paint), and it calls PriceScaleEngine.SetAuto(). That throws away
    the Manual price scale that ChartInputController sets (price-axis wheel zoom = SetManual,
    T4.07/T2.07), so price zoom is overwritten the same instant it is made.
FIX (spec T2.07: Auto/Manual price scale):
  C2.1 Run the visible-range price fit ONLY when _view.PriceScale.Fit == ScaleFit.Auto.
  C2.2 Never call SetAuto() from FitPriceToVisible/OnMouseWheel/OnMouseMove/UpdateSize.
       SetAuto is called only by an explicit user action (reset to auto), never by zoom/pan.
  C2.3 Prefer the existing PriceScaleEngine.ComputeAuto over a hand-written min/max loop.
  C2.4 Remove the fit call from UpdateSize(); size changes must keep the current price range
       (Auto mode re-fits on its own through C2.1).
  C2.5 The 300-bar clamp in ApplySettings (vis = Math.Min(vis, 300)) is NOT in the spec:
       remove it. VisibleCandles from Settings is applied as given (min 20, max = bar count).
  C2.6 TimeAxisHeight 28 -> 24 (spec ViewState value). PriceAxisWidth stays 64.
  C2.7 Tests (write first): (a) wheel on PriceAxis region then paint -> PriceScale.Fit stays
       Manual and the range is the zoomed one; (b) wheel on plot zooms time only and keeps
       the price range in Manual mode; (c) in Auto mode price follows the visible bars;
       (d) resizing the control does not reset a Manual range.
  C2.8 VISUAL gate: owner checks time zoom at cursor, price-axis zoom, drag pan, Ctrl precision
       zoom, "go to latest". Owner approves by screenshot/recording.
  If the owner still sees a defect after C2, STOP and report; do not patch around it.
